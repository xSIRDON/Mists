# Mists

A Fabric 1.20.1 mod that adds a **water-world progression system** to Minecraft. Built specifically for **maark's [True Survival](https://modrinth.com/modpack/minecraft-true-survival) modpack**.

> The player wakes on a small deserted island in a 2000-block ocean bubble. The world beyond is hidden behind a wall of mist. Growing in strength via the LevelZ skill system pushes the mist back; reaching the highest tier reveals the vanilla world that lay beyond all along.

---

## What it does

- **Modifies Minecraft's own noise router** so that within a 2000-block radius of world origin (0, 0), the chunk generator produces deep ocean — regardless of seed. Outside that bubble, vanilla worldgen runs entirely unmodified.
- **Materializes a single deterministic island** inside the bubble (200–800 blocks from origin, seeded from world seed). The island IS vanilla terrain — generated by Minecraft's chunk generator with vanilla surface rules, vanilla caves, vanilla features, vanilla biomes — just shaped by an added density contribution that turns ~50 blocks of ocean into land at the deterministic position.
- **Adds a custom biome** (`mists:mists_open_ocean`) that the bubble's chunks resolve to — atmospheric water colours, reduced mob spawns, vanilla-style seafloor decoration via datapack features.
- **Surrounds each player with a personal mist boundary** keyed to their LevelZ total level. The boundary uses real fog rendering (Minecraft's terrain-fog system) plus hostile-waters debuffs and a hard movement clamp. As the player levels up, the mist retreats; at level 30 it disappears entirely and the rest of the world opens up.

---

## How it actually works (the Terralith-style approach)

The mod is a hybrid of **mixin-driven worldgen** + **declarative datapack content**:

| Layer | What it does |
|---|---|
| `NoiseConfigMixin` | `@Redirect`s the call to `NoiseRouter.apply(visitor)` inside `NoiseConfig.<init>`. Wraps the unapplied router with bubble bias on `continents`, `finalDensity`, and `initialDensityWithoutJaggedness`, then hands the wrapped tree to vanilla's seed-injection visitor. The visited+wrapped router becomes the chunk generator's source of truth — biomes flow from wrapped continents, terrain shape from wrapped finalDensity. |
| `MultiNoiseBiomeSourceMixin` | When biome selection would return any biome inside the bubble, swap to the biome listed under the `mists:bubble_biomes` datapack tag (defaults to `mists:mists_open_ocean`). |
| `BackgroundRendererMixin` | Closes vanilla terrain fog inward as the player approaches the boundary. No particles, no toggle — a continuous smoothstep ramp using Minecraft's own fog system. |
| `EnhancedCelestialsForecastFixMixin` | Suppresses a pre-existing crash in Enhanced Celestials' empty-forecast guard that the True Survival modpack hits independently of Mists. |
| Density functions (`BubbleBiasFunction`, `MistsIslandDensityFunction`) | Position-based density math that JSON can't express. Combined with vanilla density functions via `DensityFunctionTypes.add` / `.max`. |
| Datapack JSON | The biome itself, configured/placed features (driftwood patches, dense seagrass, bone scatter, weathered stone, kelp clusters), the `bubble_biomes` tag, the `mists:tall_kelp` configured-feature instances. |
| Custom `Feature<TallKelpFeatureConfig>` | Vanilla `minecraft:kelp` can't be made taller than ~8 blocks. We register a custom feature type and instance it via datapack to produce kelp towers of 12–24 blocks. |

The same composition pattern Terralith uses — vanilla's noise pipeline + composable density bias + datapack content — applied to a single bubble around origin instead of replacing the entire overworld.

## Progression tiers

| Tier | Required Level | Mist radius from spawn | What you can reach |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | island radius + ~25 blocks (typically ~50) | Spawn island only |
| 2 | 5 | 350 blocks | Open ocean around the island |
| 3 | 10 | 650 blocks | More open ocean |
| 4 | 15 | 1000 blocks | Half the bubble |
| 5 | 30 | ∞ (disabled) | Past the bubble into vanilla worldgen |

Tier 1's radius is **dynamic** — it wraps the actual measured size of the spawn island plus a small ocean buffer, so the initial boundary feels right regardless of how the seed shaped the island.

## The mist (three layers)

The boundary is **not** a vanilla world border. It is a layered system:

1. **Real terrain fog** — vanilla's fog start/end distances are clamped inward as the player approaches the boundary, fading from normal fog at 240 blocks out to dense white right at the wall. Uses Minecraft's own renderer; no particle spam. No on/off — a continuous smoothstep ramp.
2. **Hostile waters** — a band just inside the wall where players take escalating Slowness, Nausea, and drowning damage. Brief exploration possible, sustained pushing is fatal.
3. **Invisible wall** — a hard server-side movement clamp. Boats stop, swimming stops, walking stops, falling stops. Enderpearls and chorus-fruit teleports that would cross the boundary are cancelled and refunded.

The boundary is a **full vertical column** from `y=-64` to `y=320`. Tunneling under and flying over are both blocked. The clamp **bypasses entirely** for players in Creative or Spectator gamemode.

---

## Multiplayer

- **Per-player progression.** Each player has their own mist radius driven by their own LevelZ level. Two players on the same server at different levels are blocked by different boundaries.
- A higher-level player can sail to the far reaches alone. A lower-level passenger sharing their boat causes the boat to stop at the *lower* player's boundary (lowest-common-denominator for vehicles and mounts).
- Players are never hidden from each other — only the local fog effect is per-viewer.

## Architecture

### Worldgen subsystem (server-only)
- `MistsIslandConfig.deriveFromSeed(...)` — pure function: world seed → island centre coordinates, radii, height/depth profile.
- `NoiseConfigMixin` — wraps the noise router at construction time, before vanilla's seed-injection visitor runs. The visitor walks our wrapped tree and seeds every leaf correctly. Result: a router that produces the bubble + island as vanilla terrain.
- `MistsIslandRegistry` — per-seed config cache. Hot path: density functions read from here per sample, with a volatile version counter to invalidate when registries change.
- `MultiNoiseBiomeSourceMixin` — swaps in `mists:mists_open_ocean` for biome lookups inside the bubble.

### Boundary subsystem (server-only)
- `BoundarySystem` — per-tick handler. Reads each player's LevelZ level, computes their mist radius, classifies their position into one of (safe / hostile waters / wall), applies effects.
- `PearlClamp` / `VehicleClamp` / `PlayerJoinClamp` — bypass-prevention handlers for the obvious vectors.
- `MistRadiusPayload` — S2C packet. Sent on tier transitions; carries radius, animation source, centre coords, tier ordinal.

### Client subsystem
- `MistState` — client-side cache of the current radius, animated source, and centre coords. Smoothstep-eased over 3 seconds when the server sends an update.
- `BackgroundRendererMixin` — reads `MistState` and modifies vanilla fog accordingly.
- `TierUnlockToast` — shows a title + chat message + sound when the player crosses a tier threshold.

### Datapack content (loaded at world creation)
- `data/mists/worldgen/biome/mists_open_ocean.json` — the biome
- `data/mists/worldgen/configured_feature/` — 7 custom configured features (driftwood, bone scatter, dense seagrass, weathered stone, abandoned kelp, tall kelp normal/giant)
- `data/mists/worldgen/placed_feature/` — placement modifiers for each
- `data/mists/tags/worldgen/biome/bubble_biomes.json` — flexibility tag for the biome swap

The server is fully authoritative. The client only displays what the server tells it.

## LevelZ integration

- The mod **reads** each player's LevelZ total level via a reflective bridge (`LevelZBridge`). It does not write LevelZ state.
- On a level change, the player's tier is re-evaluated. If the player crosses a threshold, the server sends a `MistRadiusPayload` to that client and the new radius is animated over ~3 seconds with a title + sound cue.
- LevelZ is a hard required dependency. The mod will refuse to load without it.
- The reflective bridge is tuned for the True Survival LevelZ fork (`levelz-true-survival-*.jar`), which has a different `PlayerStatsManagerAccess` signature than upstream LevelZ.

## Bypass prevention

| Vector | Handling |
|---|---|
| Walking / swimming / boats | Server-side movement clamp on player tick |
| Falling | Vertical column extends full world height; clamp applies |
| Tunneling under | Full column extends to bedrock |
| Flying / Elytra | Same clamp regardless of `y` |
| Enderpearls | Cancelled and refunded if destination crosses the boundary |
| Chorus fruit | Random teleport destination clamped to allowed zone |
| Ridden mobs | Lowest-level rider's radius applies |
| Multi-passenger boats | Lowest-level passenger's radius applies |
| Creative / Spectator gamemode | Bypass everything — no clamp, no debuffs, no fog overlay |
| `/tp`, `/spreadplayers`, KubeJS scripts | Not blocked — admins/server scripts can bypass intentionally |

## Compatibility

Designed to coexist cleanly with the True Survival modpack's complete mod list:

- **LevelZ** (`levelz-true-survival-1.4.13`) — required, the progression source
- **ToughAsNails** — thirst/temperature unaffected; mist hostile-waters effect stacks naturally
- **HardcoreRevival** — death and K.O. handled vanilla; respawn returns to spawn island with whatever mist radius the player's level dictates
- **Enhanced Celestials** — Mists ships a tiny compatibility mixin that silences EC's empty-forecast crash (a pre-existing issue in the modpack independent of Mists)
- **Biome Makeover** — biomes generate normally beyond the bubble; Mists Open Ocean is the only biome inside the bubble
- **SereneSeasons** — season effects propagate normally across the world
- **Sodium / Lithium / FerriteCore / EntityCulling** — fog rendering uses standard Fabric API hooks; no expected conflict

No worldgen content is modified outside the bubble. Beyond ~2200 blocks from origin (the bubble's fade band), the seed runs as if Mists were not installed.

---

## Death and respawn

- Vanilla and HardcoreRevival handle death entirely. This mod does not override death behavior.
- On respawn at world spawn, the player reappears on the spawn island with whatever mist radius their current level dictates.
- If vanilla's "find safe spawn" places the player outside the boundary on join, `PlayerJoinClamp` teleports them back to spawn — Creative/Spectator players are exempt.
- LevelZ progress is preserved through death (vanilla LevelZ behavior).

## Configuration via datapack

Server admins can override mod behavior without a new build by overriding datapack files:

- **Add biomes to the bubble** — extend `data/mists/tags/worldgen/biome/bubble_biomes.json` with more biome IDs. The biome-source swap picks from the tag.
- **Add features to the biome** — append placed features to `data/mists/worldgen/biome/mists_open_ocean.json` via a datapack overlay.
- **Customize feature behavior** — override any `mists:configured_feature/*` or `mists:placed_feature/*` file with different parameters (counts, heights, block lists).
- **Disable the density-function bubble entirely** — set `MistsIslandRegistry.setEnabled(false)` in a server-side mod that loads before Mists. The mod falls back to a runtime block-placement path (legacy v0.13 codepath, kept for safety).
